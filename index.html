What we believe

The Opping
Manifesto.

We believe the world doesn’t need more stuff.

It needs to make better use of the things it already has.

Every day, perfectly good products are donated with the hope they’ll help someone else. Too often, they remain unseen.

Not because nobody wanted them.
Because nobody could find them.

We believe buying second-hand should be as easy as buying new.

We believe charities should spend more time helping communities than creating listings.

We believe families shouldn’t have to choose between affordability and sustainability.

We believe good things deserve another life.

The most sustainable product isn’t the next one we make.
It’s the one we’ve already made.

That’s why we built Opping.
